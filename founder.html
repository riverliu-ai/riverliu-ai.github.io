What I am building

My current focus is an AI audio assistant for high-value sales and delivery scenarios. The goal is simple: capture what people actually said, remember what matters, and turn conversations into follow-up, structure, and action.

Why this matters

In real business, people do not lose deals because they lack data. They lose deals because context disappears, intent gets forgotten, and follow-through breaks. I want to build systems that turn messy human interaction into usable memory and better execution.
